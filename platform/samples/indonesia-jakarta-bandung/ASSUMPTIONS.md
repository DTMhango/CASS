# Jakarta–Bandung test portfolio: what it is and what was assumed

This portfolio is invented. It exists to exercise the platform end to end for
Indonesia, not to represent any real book, and every figure below is a testing
assumption rather than an underwriting position.

Generated by `make_portfolio.py` with a fixed seed, so the same files come back
byte for byte.

## The book

| | |
|---|---|
| Portfolio | `IDJB01`, "Jakarta-Bandung test book" |
| Locations | 64, in 20 places between Tangerang and Soreang |
| Accounts | 3 (residential scheme, commercial programme, industrial) |
| Policies | 4 layers across the 3 accounts |
| Total insured value | USD 1,363,055,442 |
| Currency | USD throughout — one currency, because the Oasis Financial Module does not calculate multi-currency terms |
| Peril covered | QEQ (earthquake shake) only |
| Valuation | Building, Other, Contents and BI, with BI only on the commercial and industrial accounts |

Every location sits inside the region the hazard run computed (longitude 106.5
to 107.9, latitude −7.2 to −5.9). A location outside it would key to a cell the
footprint holds no ground motion for and would silently contribute no loss.

## Why these taxonomies

Each location states `OccupancyCode`, `ConstructionCode` and `NumberOfStoreys`
chosen so the class resolves to exactly **one** intensity measure in the GEM
mapping this model version carries. 56 of the 240 GEM classes respond at several
measures, and CASS refuses those while no multi-IMT representation is approved —
so a test book drawn at random would have had a quarter of its value refused for
a governance reason rather than a modelling one.

## Insurance terms (invented)

* **Location terms.** Commercial and industrial locations carry a deductible of
  2.5% of their total insured value and a limit of 80% of it. The residential
  scheme carries none, so ground-up and insured differ for a readable reason.
* **ACC001, residential scheme.** One layer, 100% share, policy deductible USD
  250,000, policy limit USD 40m.
* **ACC002, commercial programme.** Two layers: USD 25m excess USD 5m at 100%,
  then USD 70m excess USD 30m at 50%. Policy deductible USD 1m on the first.
* **ACC003, industrial.** One layer, USD 60m excess USD 2.5m, 75% signed share,
  policy deductible USD 500,000.

## Reinsurance terms (invented)

| Contract | Type | Terms | Scope | Inuring priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quota share | 30% ceded, 100% placed | ACC002 and ACC003 | 1 |
| 2 | Per-risk excess | USD 8m excess USD 2m, 90% placed, location level | ACC003 | 2 |
| 3 layer 1 | Catastrophe excess | USD 20m excess USD 5m, 100% placed | whole portfolio | 3 |
| 3 layer 2 | Catastrophe excess | USD 50m excess USD 25m, 85% placed | whole portfolio | 3 |

All contracts incept 2026-01-01 and expire 2026-12-31, cover QEQ, and are
written in USD. Contract references are integers because OED numbers contracts
and the engine parses that column as a number — CASS now validates that too,
having first discovered it the hard way.

## What the numbers from this book do and do not mean

The model version is published as a **research prototype**: the grid version is
a draft and no end-to-end validation date has been recorded. The GEM
vulnerability functions and the PuSGeN 2024 hazard are both publicly available
from GEM, and are used under GEM Foundation's written permission. So every
result carries "not usable for decisions", which is the honest state, and the
AAL and PML below are a demonstration that the pipeline computes them, not a
view of Indonesian earthquake risk. CASS is a research tool in any case: no
result from it is a basis for pricing or reserving.

## What this book produced, end to end

Run on the Jakarta–Bandung hazard set (27,313 events over 1,000 simulated years,
962 cells, 12.76m footprint rows across PGA, SA(0.3), SA(0.6) and SA(1.0);
nothing clipped above the top intensity bin). All values USD.

| | Ground-up | Insured | Reinsurance (ceded) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average annual loss | 566,728 | 146,866 | 44,278 |
| Standard deviation | 3,348,481 | 1,812,917 | 404,173 |
| 10-year | 854,073 | 0 | 0 |
| 50-year | 5,171,734 | 956,717 | 521,745 |
| 100-year | 11,958,553 | 3,913,637 | 1,648,674 |
| 250-year | 20,948,830 | 10,018,996 | 3,255,739 |
| 1,000-year | 51,877,868 | 28,753,996 | 4,462,369 |

The shape is the one the terms imply: deductibles and limits take about
three-quarters out of the ground-up average, and the reinsurance programme —
30% quota share on two accounts, a per-risk excess on the industrial book and a
two-layer catastrophe programme attaching at USD 5m — cedes nothing at ten years
and flattens above 250 years as the second catastrophe layer exhausts.
